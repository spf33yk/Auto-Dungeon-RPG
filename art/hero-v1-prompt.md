## 生成プロンプト

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: pixel-art hero design approval sheet for an original Japanese autonomous dungeon RPG.
Primary request: ONE friendly skeleton adventurer, once a hero and now amnesiac, shown as four consistent idle directional views. A warm, approachable classic Japanese roguelike feeling, with entirely original design. Large readable round ivory skull, kind dark eye sockets, compact little ribcage, short limbs, a tiny worn muted moss-green shoulder cape, a plain brown belt, a short rusty iron sword. No hat, no helmet. Gentle and quietly determined rather than frightening.
Style/medium: strict low-resolution pixel art, each character designed on a 32 by 32 logical pixel grid, magnified with crisp nearest-neighbor square pixels. Restrained palette of ivory, muted moss green, warm brown and dark desaturated outlines. No gradients, no smooth painting, no antialiasing, no glossy 3D.
Composition: a clean 2 by 2 sheet containing ONLY the same skeleton in four neutral standing poses: top-left facing down toward viewer, top-right facing right, bottom-left facing up away from viewer, bottom-right facing left. Same proportions, same cloak, same sword and accessories in every view, consistent ground baseline per row, generous transparent margins between all four figures, fully visible feet and sword. Slight top-down orthographic game perspective. No additional characters, no scene, no text or labels, no arrows, no grid lines, no watermark.
Background: truly transparent alpha. This is a preview for art direction approval rather than a finished animation sheet.
