Fill each sprite about 80 percent of its cell, all objects fully contained with generous clear padding. BOTTOM MIDDLE: an upright rounded grey crypt gravestone on a small stone base, its front visible from the slight overhead game view; no readable inscription or cross. BOTTOM RIGHT: a compatible cracked gravestone variant, same scale and silhouette. Match the supplied stone tile palette and atmosphere: muted grey, moss green, worn brown, bone beige, restrained dull gold. Classic low-resolution crisp pixel art, restrained flat-color clusters, strong readable silhouettes and dark outlines. Slight overhead orthographic game view, not isometric. No realistic painting, no smooth shading, no antialiasing, no bloom, no external cast shadow, no characters, no decorative illustration background.
